## Overview

EduStream uses a secure email and password authentication system to protect your account. Once logged in, you'll have access to all platform features including live streams, groups, and direct calls.

## Signing In

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Login Page">
    Open EduStream in your web browser. You'll be greeted with the login screen featuring:
    - A user avatar icon at the top
    - Email and password input fields
    - A "Show password" toggle button
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Your Credentials">
    Fill in your login information:
    - **Email**: Enter your registered email address
    - **Password**: Type your password securely
    
    <Tip>
      Click the eye icon next to the password field to toggle password visibility.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Sign In">
    Press the "Ingresar" (Sign In) button to authenticate. The button will show a loading spinner while processing your request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens After Login

Once you successfully authenticate:

1. **Token Storage**: A secure authentication token is stored locally in your browser
2. **User Profile**: Your user information is cached for quick access
3. **Socket Connection**: A real-time WebSocket connection is established for live features
4. **Redirect**: You're automatically redirected to the Dashboard

<Info>
  Your session remains active until you explicitly log out or your authentication token expires.
</Info>

## Troubleshooting Login Issues

### Error Messages

If you encounter login problems, you'll see an error alert at the top of the form:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Account Locked" icon="lock">
    Your account may be temporarily locked. Contact your administrator to unlock it.
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Invalid Credentials" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Double-check your email and password. Remember that passwords are case-sensitive.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Common Solutions

<Accordion title="Clear Browser Cache">
  Sometimes cached data can interfere with login. Try clearing your browser's cache and cookies, then attempt to sign in again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Check Network Connection">
  Ensure you have a stable internet connection. Login requires communication with the EduStream servers.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Browser Compatibility">
  EduStream works best with modern browsers like Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge. Make sure your browser is up to date.
</Accordion>

## Security Features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Encrypted Connection" icon="shield-halved">
    All authentication traffic is encrypted using HTTPS to protect your credentials.
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Session Management" icon="clock">
    Your session is securely managed with JWT tokens that expire automatically.
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Account Status" icon="user-check">
    Administrators can lock/unlock accounts to maintain platform security.
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Auto-Logout" icon="arrow-right-from-bracket">
    Inactive sessions are automatically terminated after a period of inactivity.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
