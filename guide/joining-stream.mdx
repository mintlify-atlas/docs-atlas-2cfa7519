## Overview

As a viewer on EduStream, you can join live streaming sessions to watch educational content, interact with hosts, and collaborate with other participants.

## Finding Streams to Join

There are several ways to discover and join live streams:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Dashboard" icon="house">
    View all active streams in the "Transmisiones en Vivo" section on your dashboard.
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Group Invitations" icon="envelope">
    Receive stream invitations shared in your groups.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Joining a Stream

### From the Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse Active Streams">
    On your dashboard, scroll to the "Transmisiones en Vivo" section to see all currently live streams.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Click a Stream Card">
    Click on any stream card showing:
    - Red "EN VIVO" badge
    - Stream name
    - Host name
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirm Join">
    A modal appears asking "¿Ingresar a la reunión?" with:
    - Stream details
    - "Cancelar" (Cancel) button
    - "Unirse ahora" (Join now) button
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Wait for Admission">
    After clicking "Unirse ahora":
    - Your join request is sent to the host
    - You'll see an "Iniciando reunión" (Starting meeting) loading screen
    - Wait for the host to admit you from the waiting room
  </Step>
</Steps>

### From a Group Invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Group Chat">
    Navigate to the Groups section and open a group where a stream was shared.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Locate Stream Invitation">
    Look for a message with:
    - Blue background
    - "INVITACION" heading
    - "Unirse a la sala" (Join the room) button
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Click Join Button">
    Click "Unirse a la sala" to open the stream join modal.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirm and Connect">
    Click "Unirse ahora" in the confirmation modal to request access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The host controls who can join the stream. You may need to wait in the waiting room until admitted.
</Info>

## Viewer Interface

Once admitted to a stream, you'll see:

### Main Video Area

The center screen displays:

- **Host's video**: The host's camera or screen share
- **Stream timer**: Duration in the top-left corner
- **Participant count**: Number of viewers in the top-right
- **Your camera thumbnail**: Small picture-in-picture view (bottom-right)

### Your Camera View

In the bottom-right corner:

- Shows your camera feed (if enabled)
- Labeled "Tú" (You)
- Microphone status indicator (green/red)
- Click to toggle between viewing yourself or the host full-screen

<Note>
  If your camera is off, you'll see a user icon placeholder instead of your video feed.
</Note>

## Viewer Controls

At the bottom center, you have access to several controls:

### Audio Control

<Accordion title="Toggle Microphone">
  Click the microphone button to:
  - **Gray background**: Microphone is active
  - **Red background**: Microphone is muted
  
  <Tip>
    Keep your microphone muted when not speaking to avoid background noise.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

### Video Control

<Accordion title="Toggle Camera">
  Click the camera button to:
  - **Camera icon**: Video is active (others can see you)
  - **Slashed camera icon**: Video is off
  
  Your thumbnail updates automatically to show on/off status.
</Accordion>

### Screen Sharing

<Accordion title="Share Your Screen">
  As a viewer, you can share your screen:
  1. Click the monitor/desktop icon
  2. Select what to share (screen, window, or tab)
  3. Others will see your shared content
  4. Click again to stop sharing
  
  <Info>
    Screen sharing is useful for presentations or demonstrations when the host gives you the floor.
  </Info>
</Accordion>

### Additional Controls

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Expand/Collapse" icon="arrows-left-right">
    Toggle the side panels on/off to maximize video area (desktop only).
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Leave Stream" icon="arrow-right-from-bracket">
    Click the X button to exit the stream at any time.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Mobile Viewing

On mobile devices, additional buttons appear:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Chat Button">
    Tap the chat bubble icon to open the chat panel.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Interact with Chat">
    Send messages, view participants, and interact with the stream chat.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Close Chat">
    Tap outside the chat panel or the overlay to close it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Viewing Other Participants

When multiple viewers are in the stream:

### Participant Panel (Desktop)

On the right side, you'll see:

- **"Otros participantes" section**: Shows all other viewers
- **Participant thumbnails**: Small video feeds
- **Names**: Displayed below each thumbnail
- **Audio indicators**: Microphone status for each person

<Tip>
  Click any participant's thumbnail to view them in the main video area.
</Tip>

### Switching Views

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click a Thumbnail">
    Click any participant thumbnail in the right panel.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="View Full Screen">
    That participant's video appears in the main area.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Return to Host">
    Click the host's thumbnail or double-click the main area to return to the host's view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Chat Functionality

When the chat panel is expanded:

### Sending Messages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type Your Message">
    Enter your message in the text input at the bottom of the chat panel.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Send Message">
    Press Enter or click the send button (paper airplane icon).
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="View in Chat">
    Your message appears in the chat stream with your name and timestamp.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Chat messages are visible to all participants in the stream.
</Note>

## Stream Events

Be aware of these important events:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Host Disconnects" icon="wifi-slash">
    If the host loses connection, you may need to wait for them to reconnect.
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Stream Ends" icon="stop">
    When the host ends the stream, you'll be notified and redirected.
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="You're Expelled" icon="user-slash">

  </Card>

## Leaving a Stream

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Leave Button">
    Click the X button in the controls area.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirm Exit">
    A modal asks "¿Estás seguro de que deseas salir?" (Are you sure you want to leave?)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirm Leave">
    Click "Sí" (Yes) to disconnect and return to the platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Leaving a stream notifies the host and removes you from the participant list.
</Info>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="Can't Join Stream">
  - Ensure you have a stable internet connection
  - Check that the stream is still active
  - Verify your browser has permission to access camera/microphone
  - Try refreshing and joining again
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can't See Video">
  - Check if your camera is enabled (not showing slashed icon)
  - Ensure no other application is using your camera
  - Verify browser permissions for camera access
  - Try toggling the camera off and on again
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Audio Not Working">
  - Check if your microphone is muted (red background)
  - Ensure your browser has microphone permissions
  - Test your microphone in another application
  - Check your operating system's audio settings
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Poor Video Quality">
  - Check your internet connection speed
  - Close other bandwidth-intensive applications
  - Move closer to your WiFi router
  - Consider turning off your camera to reduce bandwidth usage
</Accordion>
