---
title: Profile Settings
description: Manage your EduStream profile information, including your nickname and profile photo
---

The Profile Settings page allows you to customize your personal information and appearance in EduStream.

## Accessing Profile Settings

Navigate to the Settings section and select the **Profile** tab to manage your profile information.

## Available Settings

### Profile Photo

Your profile photo is displayed throughout EduStream to help others identify you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload a new photo">
    Click **Change photo** below your current profile picture and select an image file from your device. Supported formats include JPG, PNG, and other common image types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview your photo">
    After selecting a file, you'll see a preview of your new profile photo before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove your photo">
    Click **Delete photo** to remove your current profile picture. EduStream will generate a default avatar based on your nickname.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  If you delete your profile photo, EduStream automatically generates a colorful avatar using your nickname initials.
</Info>

### Nickname

Your nickname is how you appear to other users in EduStream.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your nickname">
    Type your preferred display name in the **Name** field. This field is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click the **Save** button to update your profile with the new nickname.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Your nickname will be visible to other users based on your privacy settings. Choose a name you're comfortable sharing.
</Warning>

### Email Address

Your email address is displayed in the profile settings but cannot be modified from this page. This is your account's primary identifier.

<Note>
  The email field is read-only to maintain account security and integrity.
</Note>

## Saving Your Changes

After making any changes to your profile:

1. Review your modifications
2. Click the **Save** button at the bottom of the form
3. You'll receive a confirmation notification when your profile is successfully updated

<Info>
  All profile changes are saved to your account and will persist across sessions and devices.
</Info>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="Changes aren't saving">
  Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly. The nickname field must not be empty. If the issue persists, check your internet connection and try again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Profile photo not uploading">
  Make sure your image file is in a supported format (JPG, PNG, etc.) and is not corrupted. Try selecting a different image or reducing the file size.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Default avatar not updating">
  If you delete your photo, the default avatar is generated based on your current nickname. Make sure you've saved your changes and refresh the page.
</Accordion>
