## Overview

EduStream allows you to host live video streaming sessions for educational purposes. As a host, you control participant access, manage audio/video settings, share your screen, and record sessions.

## Starting a Stream

Before starting your stream, ensure you have:

- A working camera and microphone
- Stable internet connection
- Browser permissions for camera/microphone access

<Note>
  When you start a stream, the platform will request permission to access your camera and microphone. You must allow these permissions to proceed.
</Note>

## Managing Participants

### Waiting Room

When viewers request to join, they appear in the "Sala de espera" (Waiting Room):

<Steps>
  <Step title="View Pending Requests">
    The left panel shows users waiting to join with their names.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Admit or Reject">
    For each waiting user, you have two options:
    - **Green checkmark**: Admit the user to the stream
    - **Red X**: Reject the join request
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="User Joins">
    Once admitted, the user appears in "En la reunión" (In the meeting) list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Participant Controls

For participants already in the stream:

<Accordion title="View Participant Status">
  Each participant shows:
  - Name or email
  - Microphone status (green if active, red if muted)
  - Three-dot menu for actions
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Available Actions">
  Click the three-dot menu next to a participant to:
  - **Silenciar** (Mute): Mute their microphone
  - **Expulsar** (Expel): Remove them from the stream
</Accordion>

## Stream Controls

At the bottom center of the screen, you'll find essential streaming controls:

### Audio/Video Controls

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Microphone" icon="microphone">
    Toggle your microphone on/off
    
    - Gray background: Microphone active
    - Red background: Microphone muted
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Camera" icon="video">
    Toggle your camera on/off
    
    - Video icon: Camera active
    - Slashed video icon: Camera off
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Screen Sharing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Screen Share Button">
    Click the monitor/desktop icon to start sharing your screen.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Select What to Share">
    Your browser will prompt you to choose:
    - Entire screen
    - Application window
    - Browser tab
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Share Active">
    When sharing:
    - The button turns blue
    - Your camera feed moves to a small picture-in-picture window
    - Participants see your shared screen
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Stop Sharing">
    Click the button again or use your browser's stop sharing button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Screen sharing is ideal for presentations, demonstrations, and educational content.
</Tip>

## Recording Your Stream

EduStream integrates with api.video for professional stream recording:

### Starting a Recording

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Screen Sharing First">
    You must be sharing your screen before starting a recording.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Click Record Button">
    The record button shows:
    - White circle icon when not recording
    - Stop icon when recording is active
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Recording Active">
    When recording:
    - Button turns red
    - Screen content and audio are captured
    - Recording continues even if you toggle camera/mic
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Stopping and Accessing Recording

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stop Recording">
    Click the red record button to stop.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Upload Progress">
    A modal shows upload progress:
    - Progress bar (0-100%)
    - Upload status message
    - Cancel option (if needed)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Recording Ready">
    When complete, a modal displays:
    - Video player URL
    - Embed code (iframe)
    - Copy link button
    - Video preview
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Recordings are automatically uploaded to api.video and stored for future access.
</Info>

## Stream Options Menu

Click the three-dot menu in the top-left participant panel to access:

<Accordion title="End Transmission">
  **Finalizar transmisión** (End transmission)
  - Stops the stream for all participants
  - Closes all connections
  - Updates stream status to "finished"
  - Redirects to transmissions list
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Share Stream">
  **Compartir** (Share)
  - Opens a modal to share the stream link
  - Search for groups
  - Select multiple groups
  - Send invitation messages
</Accordion>

### Ending Your Stream

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click End Stream">
    Select "Finalizar transmisión" from the options menu.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Confirm Action">
    A confirmation modal appears asking if you're sure.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Stream Ends">
    Upon confirmation:
    - All participants are disconnected
    - Media streams are stopped
    - Recording link is saved (if recorded)
    - You're redirected to the transmissions page
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Video Display Features

### Main Video Area

The center screen displays:

- **Your stream**: By default, shows your camera or screen share
- **Participant stream**: Click any participant thumbnail to show them full-screen
- **Timer**: Live stream duration in the top-left
- **Viewer count**: Number of active participants in the top-right

<Tip>
  Double-click the main video area to return to your own stream view.
</Tip>

### Participant Thumbnails

The right panel shows mini video feeds:

- **Host thumbnail**: Shows you (marked as "Tú (Host)")
- **Viewer thumbnails**: All admitted participants
- **Audio indicators**: Microphone icon showing mute status
- **Screen share indicator**: Blue monitor icon when sharing

<Note>
  Click any thumbnail to display that participant's video in the main area.
</Note>

## Mobile Streaming

On mobile devices:

- Tap the **user group icon** to show/hide participants panel
- Tap the **chat icon** to show/hide chat
- Panels appear as overlays on smaller screens
- Tap outside panels to dismiss them

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="Camera/Microphone Not Working">
  1. Check browser permissions in your address bar
  2. Ensure no other application is using your camera
  3. Try refreshing the page and allowing access again
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Screen Share Not Starting">
  1. Make sure you selected a screen/window in the browser prompt
  2. Check if you have permission to share in your operating system
  3. Close and reopen the sharing dialog
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Recording Failed">
  1. Ensure screen sharing was active before recording
  2. Check your internet connection stability
  3. Try stopping and starting the recording again
</Accordion>
