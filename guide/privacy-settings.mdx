title: Privacy Settings
description: Control your visibility, online status, and notification preferences in EduStream
---

The Privacy Settings page gives you control over how you appear to other users and how you receive notifications in EduStream.

## Accessing Privacy Settings

Navigate to the Settings section and select the **Privacy** tab to manage your privacy and notification preferences.

## Privacy Options

### Online Status

Control whether other users can see when you're actively using EduStream.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Toggle online status">
    Use the toggle switch next to **Online status** to enable or disable your visibility.
  </Step>
  <Step title="When enabled">
    Other users will be able to see when you're active on the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="When disabled">
    Your online status will be hidden from other users, giving you more privacy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Even when your online status is hidden, your activity and interactions on the platform will still function normally.
</Info>

### Profile Visibility

Decide who can view your profile and activity on EduStream.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select visibility level">
    Choose from the dropdown menu next to **Profile visibility**:
    - **Everyone**: All users can view your profile
    - **Contacts only**: Only your contacts can see your profile
    - **Nobody**: Your profile is private and hidden from all users
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your preference">
    Click the **Save** button to apply your visibility settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Setting your profile visibility to "Nobody" will make your profile completely private. Other users won't be able to see your information or activity.
</Warning>

## Notification Settings

### Push Notifications

Manage whether you receive notifications for platform activity.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Toggle notifications">
    Use the toggle switch next to **Notifications** to enable or disable alerts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="When enabled">
    You'll receive notifications about relevant activity, messages, and updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="When disabled">
    All platform notifications will be turned off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Disabling notifications means you won't receive alerts about messages, mentions, or other platform activities. You'll need to check the platform manually for updates.
</Note>

## Saving Your Changes

After adjusting your privacy and notification settings:

1. Review all your selections
2. Click the **Save** button at the bottom of the page
3. You'll see a confirmation message when your settings are successfully updated

<Info>
  Your privacy settings take effect immediately after saving and apply across all your devices.
</Info>

## Understanding Privacy Levels

<Accordion title="Everyone">
  When you set your profile visibility to "Everyone", all EduStream users can view your profile information and activity. This is the most open setting and is ideal for users who want maximum visibility on the platform.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Contacts only">
  This option restricts profile viewing to users you've added as contacts. It provides a balance between privacy and networking, allowing you to share information with trusted connections while maintaining privacy from others.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Nobody">
  The most private option. When selected, your profile and activity are hidden from all other users. This is useful if you want to browse and use EduStream without sharing your information.
</Accordion>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="Settings not saving">
  Ensure you have a stable internet connection and that you've clicked the **Save** button after making changes. If problems persist, try refreshing the page and applying your settings again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Still receiving notifications after disabling">
  Clear your browser cache and check that the notification toggle is set to off. Some notifications may have been queued before you disabled the setting.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Profile still visible with privacy settings">
  After changing your profile visibility, it may take a few moments for the changes to propagate. Log out and log back in to ensure settings are applied. If your profile remains visible, verify that you saved your changes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can't change online status">
  Make sure the toggle switch is functioning correctly. Try clicking it multiple times and ensure you save your changes. If the issue continues, try using a different browser.
</Accordion>
