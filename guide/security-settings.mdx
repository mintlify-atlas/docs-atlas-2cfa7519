The Security Settings page helps you maintain the security of your EduStream account through password management.

## Accessing Security Settings

Navigate to the Settings section and select the **Security** tab to manage your account security.

## Change Your Password

Regularly updating your password is an important security practice. Follow these steps to change your password:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter current password">
    In the **Current password** field, type your existing password to verify your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new password">
    Enter your new password in the **New password** field. Your password must be between 8 and 15 characters long.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm new password">
    Re-type your new password in the **Confirm new password** field to ensure accuracy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click the **Save** button to update your password. You'll see a confirmation notification when the change is successful.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  After changing your password, you'll remain logged in on your current device, but may need to re-authenticate on other devices.
</Warning>

## Password Requirements

When creating a new password for your EduStream account:

- **Minimum length**: 8 characters
- **Maximum length**: 15 characters
- **Required fields**: All password fields must be filled out

<Info>
  For optimal security, use a combination of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters in your password.
</Info>

## Security Best Practices

<Accordion title="Create a strong password">
  Choose a password that is difficult to guess. Avoid using common words, personal information, or sequential patterns. Consider using a passphrase or password manager to generate and store complex passwords.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Keep your password private">
  Never share your EduStream password with anyone, including friends, family, or support staff. Legitimate EduStream staff will never ask for your password.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Update your password regularly">
  Change your password periodically, especially if you suspect unauthorized access to your account or if you've used the same password on other platforms that may have been compromised.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Use unique passwords">
  Don't reuse your EduStream password on other websites or services. Each account should have its own unique password to prevent credential stuffing attacks.
</Accordion>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="Current password is incorrect">
  If you receive an error that your current password is incorrect, double-check that you're entering it correctly. Passwords are case-sensitive. If you've forgotten your password, use the password recovery option on the login page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="New passwords don't match">
  Ensure that the text you enter in the **New password** and **Confirm new password** fields is identical. Re-type both fields carefully.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Password doesn't meet requirements">
  Verify that your new password is between 8 and 15 characters long. Passwords outside this range will not be accepted.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="All fields are required error">
  You must complete all three password fields (current password, new password, and confirm new password) before submitting the form.
</Accordion>
